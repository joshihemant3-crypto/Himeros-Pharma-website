Handbook of Sexual Medicine

By Tulika Collections · Second Edition

Handbook of Sexual Medicine is a comprehensive clinical reference covering the fundamentals of human sexual physiology and the complexities of sexual pathology. It explores sexual response in men and women, male and female sexual dysfunctions, and conditions such as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, erectile dysfunction, premature and delayed ejaculation, dyspareunia, vaginismus, and other sexual concerns. The book also examines the psychological, biological, relationship and situational factors contributing to sexual dysfunction. With its structured approach to understanding sexual function and dysfunction, it serves as a valuable resource for understanding and addressing diverse sexual health concerns.

1st Edition
Sex Therapy Clinician's Guide

Sex Therapy: A Clinician's Guide

By Tulika Collections · First Edition

Sex Therapy: A Clinician's Guide serves as a practical, structured resource for practitioners navigating sexual health interventions. Beginning with foundational concepts and clinical practice guidelines—including techniques like sensate focus—the book systematically addresses specific male and female sexual dysfunctions, such as erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, vaginismus, and arousal disorders. Beyond symptom management, it provides valuable guidance on navigating therapy obstacles, handling termination, assessing treatment outcomes, and maintaining ethical standards in practice. Designed as an actionable manual, it offers clinicians a comprehensive roadmap to guide patients toward improved sexual wellness and therapeutic success.

Clinical Manual

Sexual Facets of Medical Practice

By Tulika Collections · Medical Reference

Sexual Facets of Medical Practice is a comprehensive medical reference that explores the relationship between sexual health and a wide range of medical conditions. The book examines sexual dysfunction across cardiovascular, respiratory, neurological, urological, gynecological, endocrine, psychiatric, dermatological and other conditions, while also addressing sexuality in ageing, cancer, diabetes, fertility, sexually transmitted infections and treatment-related sexual dysfunction. By bringing sexuality into the broader context of clinical medicine, the book highlights the importance of recognising, understanding and appropriately addressing sexual health concerns as an integral part of patient care. Highly recommended must read for all the practioners dealing with sexual health and illness for a better understanding.
