Featured Medical Publications

Sexual Medicine Clinical Reference

Handbook of Sexual Medicine

By Tulika Collections · Second Edition

Handbook of Sexual Medicine is a comprehensive clinical reference covering human sexual physiology and the complexities of sexual pathology. It explores sexual response, dysfunctions, HSDD, erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, and vaginismus.

Sex Therapy Clinician's Guide

Sex Therapy: A Clinician's Guide

By Tulika Collections · First Edition

A practical, structured resource for practitioners navigating sexual health interventions. Includes sensate focus techniques, systematic protocols for male & female dysfunctions, obstacle navigation, and ethical guidelines.

Relationships Practical Guide

Making Sense

By Tulika Collections · Relationship Guide

A thoughtful and practical guide addressing questions, doubts, and everyday dilemmas couples face before and during early marriage. Explores body awareness, intimacy, sexual expectations, and open communication.

Medical Reference Clinical Specialty

Sexual Facets of Medical Practice

By Tulika Collections · Medical Reference

Examines sexual health across cardiovascular, respiratory, neurological, urological, gynecological, endocrine, and psychiatric conditions. An essential multi-specialty reference for general medicine practitioners.

Clinical Manual Practical Guide

Clinical Methods in Sexual Medicine

By Tulika Collections · Clinical Manual

A practical manual dedicated to the art of sexual health consultation. Includes 40 carefully curated real-world case scenarios providing a virtual clinic experience for clinicians and trainees.
